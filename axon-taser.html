AXON / TASER

Brand, Product & Integrated Creative

Building a technology brand across product, advertising, print and physical experiences.

Creative Direction · Art Direction · Brand Development · Product Marketing · Advertising · Print · Experiential

The Challenge

One brand. Many touchpoints.

TASER was growing from a category defining product into a broader technology brand serving law enforcement and public safety organizations.

The creative challenge was to maintain a recognizable brand presence across an expanding product portfolio while communicating technology, performance and credibility across a wide range of customer touchpoints.

The Role

From strategy to execution.

I worked across product launches, visual identity, packaging, advertising, sales materials, trade shows and other brand communications, translating business and product objectives into cohesive creative.

01 · Brand Foundation

Creating a recognizable brand system.

The TASER identity provided the foundation for a growing portfolio of products and marketing communications. Product level branding extended that system while giving individual products a clear and recognizable presence within the larger brand.
