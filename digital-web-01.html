Website Design

the ask

Set Jet, a Scottsdale based membership private jet charter company, needed a modern, mobile first website that reflected its premium brand and streamlined the path to membership. The company requested a responsive website that showcased its private jet experience, membership benefits, destinations, and company information while driving prospective members to inquire and sign up.

the answer

I designed and developed a fully responsive website that elevated Set Jet's luxury brand while creating an intuitive user experience across all devices. The site featured engaging visuals, clear navigation, compelling content, and strategically placed calls to action that encouraged visitors to explore membership benefits, submit inquiries, and convert into new members.
